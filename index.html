About

Muhammad Rizal

I’m a full‑stack developer focused on backend architecture, clean APIs, and interfaces that make complex systems feel simple. I like turning messy problems into calm, intentional experiences — and I run on curiosity, good docs, and the occasional Spotify rabbit hole.

The journey

Right now, I’m a fullstack software engineering student at RevoU and a Computer Science major at BINUS University. Most of my time goes to designing backend architecture, modelling data, and building APIs, while keeping an eye on how those systems show up in responsive, accessible UIs.

Before code, I worked in IT and Customer Support at PT Sinergy Informasi Pratama, supporting clients like BTN, Bank BJB, and BPJS Kesehatan. VPN failures, misconfigured networks, and “why isn’t this working?” taught me how much infrastructure and clear communication really matter.

Today I ship with the same mindset: stay calm under pressure, be honest about trade‑offs, and build systems that are reliable, observable, and maintainable. Outside of work you’ll find me on a mini soccer pitch, curating playlists, or writing on Medium.
