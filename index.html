I’m an engineer who enjoys turning complex systems into interfaces that feel calm, intentional, and easy to use. Right now, I’m a fullstack software engineering student at RevoU and a Computer Science major at BINUS University. I spend most of my time designing backend architecture, modelling data, and building APIs, while also sharpening how those systems surface in responsive, accessible UIs.

Before diving into code, I worked in IT and Customer Support at PT Sinergy Informasi Pratama, a system integrator serving clients like BTN, Bank BJB, and BPJS Kesehatan. Most days meant VPN failures, misconfigured networks, and “why is this broken?” moments from users who just needed things to work. That experience taught me how much good infrastructure and clear communication matter.

Now when I build software, I bring that same mindset: stay calm under pressure, be honest about trade‑offs, and ship systems that are reliable, observable, and maintainable. I care a lot about readable code, predictable APIs, and documentation that future teammates will actually thank you for.

Outside of code, you’ll probably find me on a mini soccer field, curating new playlists, or writing on Medium about music, football, and the random coffee shops that keep me going.
